## Decode the shade names first, because that's where most people get stuck

Clinique's liquid shades use a two-part code: a letter pair and a number. **CN** means cool neutral undertone, **WN** means warm neutral. The number is the depth — low numbers are fair, high numbers are deep. The range runs from WN 01 Flax at the very light end to CN 127 Truffle at the deepest, so it is genuinely wide.

So WN 46 Golden Neutral is a medium-depth shade with a warm-leaning neutral undertone. CN 10 Alabaster is very fair with a cool-neutral undertone. Once you know your letter pair and your depth, you can move between Clinique formulas without starting over — the shade names carry across several of the liquids, which is more useful than it sounds when you're switching from a matte formula in summer to a hydrating one in winter.

Two wrinkles worth knowing:

- **The Clinical Vitamin Makeup uses a different naming system.** Instead of CN/WN codes, its 28 shades are named descriptively — Light Cool 1, Medium Cool 3, Light Medium Warm 1, Deep Cool 2, Medium Deep Warm 2, and so on. It's light coverage by design, and Clinique's logic is that sheer shades flex across more skin tones, but you can't copy your Serum Foundation code straight across. You'll need to match again.
- **A swatch on skin beats any code.** Clinique's own application advice for the Serum Foundation is to start with hair a half-pump because the formula is concentrated. Do that on your jawline in daylight, wait ten minutes, and check both in indoor light and by a window before committing to a full bottle.

## The formulas most people are actually choosing between

### Even Better Clinical Serum Foundation SPF 25 — $48

This is the one people usually mean when they say "the Clinique serum foundation." It's a full-coverage liquid with a matte finish and 24-hour wear, oil-free, and targeted at oily and combination skin. The three "serum" ingredients are what Clinique builds its marketing on: UP302 with vitamin C to reduce the look of dark spots, salicylic acid to re-texturise, and hyaluronic acid to plump.

The sunscreen here is mineral — titanium dioxide 2.4% and zinc oxide 5.9% — which matters if you're sensitive to chemical filters. The brand's consumer testing reported 91% of 227 women saying colour stayed true through the day at one week, and at eight weeks 91% seeing reduced imperfections on bare skin.

Two honest caveats. It's a matte formula, so if your skin runs dry in winter it can look tight unless you moisturise first. And full coverage plus matte plus 24-hour wear is a lot of product sitting on your face — the half-pump starting point isn't a suggestion, it's how the bottle is designed to be used.

### Even Better Clinical Vitamin Makeup SPF 45 — $48

The opposite personality. Light coverage, radiant finish, 12-hour wear, and SPF 45 built from a mix of physical and chemical sunscreens. Formula highlights are 4% vitamin B3, five forms of vitamin C, and vitamin E. Clinique positions the skin types as dry, dry combination and combination oily, and it's oil-free and waterproof.

The vitamin C angle is interesting because this is essentially the makeup version of Clinique's dark spot serum line, which is why it keeps showing up in Best of lists. The other practical draw is the SPF number: a foundation with SPF 45 is a different proposition from SPF 15 if you're actually wearing it as your daytime base. That said, a foundation is not a sunscreen substitute — you'd need a genuinely generous layer for the SPF rating to mean what it means on a sunscreen label.

### Even Better Makeup SPF 15 — $39

The cheapest liquid in the range and the one with the widest shade offering: 50 shades on the retailer listing. Medium coverage, natural finish, SPF 15, antioxidant vitamin C and E. For dry combination through oily skin types it's the safe middle option, and at $39 with that many shades it's hard to argue against as a first Clinique foundation.

The trade-off is wear time. This is not the 24-hour formula, and it's the one where long-day wear complaints show up most often in older roundups. If you need something that survives a full workday plus dinner, the Serum Foundation is the more sensible spend.

### Beyond Perfecting Foundation + Concealer — $40

One bottle, two jobs, and a sponge-tip applicator that lets you either dot it on as a concealer or blend it out as foundation. Full coverage, natural finish, roughly 12-hour wear, with bonus hydrating ingredients — one review collection flagged that some users find the consistency thicker than they'd like. It's a reasonable pick if you routinely cover dark circles and blemishes, and paying $40 for a foundation-plus-concealer combination is cheaper than buying both separately.

## The rest of the shelf, and who it's for

**Superbalanced Makeup ($42)** is the old reliable for combination and T-zone skin: medium coverage, oil-free, engineered to hydrate dry patches while absorbing oil where you need it. The limitation is shade count — 11 shades is narrow by Clinique standards, and it has no SPF, so if you want sun protection you're layering it separately.

**Stay-Matte Sheer Pressed Powder Foundation ($41)** is the powder option, in 10 shades, for touch-ups and oily-skin days that would melt a liquid formula.

**Even Better Refresh Hydrating and Repairing Makeup (around $46)** is still sold in several markets and is aimed at drier skin wanting a medium-coverage hydrating base with no SPF.

**Redness Solutions** and the **Anti-Blemish Solutions Liquid Makeup** also remain available in various regions. Neither is a headline product in every market, so check availability locally before building a routine around them — inventory for these two is uneven.

## Questions people ask before buying Clinique foundation

**Which Clinique foundation is best for oily skin?**
The Even Better Clinical Serum Foundation SPF 25. It's oil-free, matte, built for oily and combination skin, and sweat- and humidity-resistant.

**Which is best for dry skin?**
The Even Better Clinical Vitamin Makeup SPF 45 is formulated for dry, dry combination and combination oily skin with a hydrating, radiant finish. Even Better Refresh is the other hydration-focused option where it's sold.

**Do Clinique foundations oxidise?**
The Serum Foundation is specifically described as a non-oxidising formula, which is what the "91% say colour stays true throughout the day" consumer result is measuring. It doesn't mean any shade will suit you — it means the shade you match won't drift orange by afternoon.

**Are they fragrance-free?**
Yes. Fragrance-free and allergy-tested is standard across these formulas, and the vitamin makeup is additionally positioned as safe for sensitive skin, sensitive eyes and contact lens wearers.

**Is SPF 25 in a foundation enough?**
Treat it as a bonus layer rather than your sun protection. SPF ratings assume a generous, even application, and almost nobody applies foundation at that thickness. The SPF 45 formula gets you closer, but a dedicated sunscreen underneath is still the dependable option.

**Do the sizes differ between shades?**
No — the liquids are 1 oz / 30 mL regardless of shade. The only size variation in the lineup is the pressed powder, which comes as a compact rather than a bottle.
